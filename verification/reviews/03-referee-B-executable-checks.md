## 1. Exact scope and definition fidelity

The statement matches the catalogue: n>=2, every positive integer tuple d, k=sum d, over C, arbitrary independently chosen linear maps on the original k factors, and powers grouping only corresponding original factors. Natural-log exp H equals the catalogue's 2^H2. The proof strengthens the lower bound to coordinate/monomial restriction while allowing general maps in the upper bound. It neither claims finite Q=exp H nor uniform-in-d convergence.

The k=2 exception is handled correctly: positivity forces n=2,d=(1,1), giving a two-by-two permutation matrix and Q_M=Q=2^N on every power. For k>=3, the upper-bound argument retains all k original modes. The supermultiplicative-limit convention is consistent with the catalogue; the proof's nonnegative bounded-superadditive argument also makes a lower bound on any unbounded subsequence sufficient.

## 2. The diagonal slice-rank proof is correct

Section 8 of PROOF.md proves the necessary bridge to ordinary subrank, rather than assuming that a coordinate bound covers general maps.

For U,V<=C^q, take A to be the union of supports of U. Over the infinite field C, some u in U has every A-coordinate nonzero; the U=0 case is explicitly included. Multiplication by u has ambient kernel dimension q-|A|, so its restriction to V has image dimension at least dim V-q+|A|. Since dim U<=|A|,

    dim(U*V)>=dim U+dim V-q.

Iteration gives the stated lower bound on the span of coordinatewise products of several subspaces. No independence, positivity or Hermitian pairing is needed.

For any slice decomposition of Delta_q with r_i slices in mode i, let U_i annihilate all distinguished coefficient vectors in that mode under the nondegenerate ordinary bilinear pairing. Then codim U_i<=r_i and the multilinear form Delta_q vanishes on U_1 x ... x U_k. Thus the product span of U_1,...,U_(k-1) lies in U_k's annihilator. Its dimension lower bound forces q<=sum_i codim U_i<=sum_i r_i. The diagonal itself is a sum of q slices, proving sr(Delta_q)=q.

Independent linear maps send each slice to a slice or zero, so sr cannot increase under the catalogue's arbitrary restrictions. Therefore Q(T)<=sr(T) follows exactly for the ordinary definition used in the conjecture.

## 3. The finite-power entropy upper bound is exact

My independent outline obtained the same argument before reading Section 8. Give a local word w score `-log product_t p_(w_t)`. Across the k local words of any support term, the total score is `k*N*H(p)`, so one mode has score at most `N*H(p)`. The set L_N of such local words has at most `exp(N*H(p))` elements because each has product probability at least `exp(-N*H(p))` and all word probabilities sum to one.

Assign every support term to its first eligible original mode and group terms by that mode and local word. This is a valid slice decomposition with at most `k*|L_N|` slices. Combined with the diagonal lemma it proves, for every N>=1,

    Q(D_d^boxtimes N)<=k*exp(N*H(p)),

for arbitrary complex-linear maps. Taking Nth roots proves the advertised upper bound with no polynomial type-count loss. Positivity of every d_a ensures every logarithm is defined. No grouping of distinct original modes, symmetry of maps, monomial restriction, or numerical entropy approximation enters this proof.

## 4. Audit of the exact finite checks

`check_entropy.py` uses only integer arithmetic. Fixing one coordinate and dropping the last identifies the relevant zero-coordinate, zero-sum rational space with dimension k-2. In its explicitly restricted dimensions one through three, every nonzero difference-generated subspace is either a primitive difference line, a plane generated by two such lines, or the full space. Its enumeration therefore covers exactly the claimed finite collision subspaces. Quotient class sizes are cross-checked independently by difference membership.

If A is the product of quotient fiber sizes and M the actual permutation support size, the checker correctly clears logarithms from

    (1/M)log A <= r/(k-2)*(log M-H(d/k))

to

    A^(k*(k-2))*k^(k*M*r)
      <= M^(k*M*r)*(product_i d_i^d_i)^(M*r).

The support labels 0,1,... are an affine rescaling of the proof's labels and therefore preserve the relevant differences, quotient partitions and ranks. Its five wrong-rank controls use the same exact inequality validator.

`check_hashing.py` independently checks the rank-one fiber inequality with exact Fractions. Its exhaustive hashing test uses precisely all original 27 edges, all six full-joint-type candidates and all 54 ordered candidate/collider pairs sharing at least one local word. A pair appears once even if it shares more than one mode. In the three-mode case a distinct shared-coordinate pair has difference rank one, justifying the expected pair count. The final restriction validator uses the full original support, not only the candidate set or the survivor set. Isolated candidates cannot share any coordinate because the pair list includes every surviving candidate against every other surviving edge; hence the checked final induced support is a diagonal.

The q=11 regression is a finite exact example, not an invocation of the proof's conservative universal prime threshold. Its probabilities are independently counted by exhaustion. The checkers make no claim to prove the all-d entropy lemma or asymptotic rate by finite enumeration.

The runner propagates -O to both child scripts and requires their nonzero exit statuses to fail. All actual acceptance guards use explicit exceptions, with no removable assert or floating-point tolerance. Code and exact output hashes are retained below.

## 6. Two independent reviewer corruptions

The original files remain untouched. Each corruption was executed on a separate corrupted copy, with exact stderr and exit status checked, both normally and under -O with -I -S -B.

1. **Merged line cosets.** Replace the line branch of `quotient_key` by just `(s[0],)`, incorrectly merging all distinct cosets of that line within a fixed-coordinate fiber. The independent difference-membership check rejects with exactly `quotient class/membership mismatch`. This tests an incorrect computed quotient, rather than reusing the author's wrong-rank corruption.
2. **Wrong hash denominator inverse.** Replace `inv2=pow(2,-1,q)` by `inv2=1`, breaking the common-hash affine identity while preserving the same support and average-free set. The exhaustive test rejects with exactly `average-free filter did not force equal hashes`. This tests the hash formula itself, rather than changing B or omitting original colliders.

All four corrupted processes exit one, have empty stdout, and give their stated exact guard message. Normal and -O agree. These tests supplement, rather than replace, the mathematical source audit.
